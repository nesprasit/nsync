NSync — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-18

NSync is an Obsidian plugin that syncs your own vault between your own devices using your own Google Drive.

What data NSync accesses

  • NSync requests only the drive.appdata scope. It can read and write only the hidden, app-specific folder it creates in your Google Drive. It cannot see any other files in your Drive.
  • The files it stores there are the notes and attachments from your Obsidian vault.

Where your data goes

  • Your data goes directly between your device and Google Drive. NSync has no server and the developer never receives your notes, files, or account information.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on your device, in the plugin's local settings, and are never uploaded.
  • NSync does not collect analytics or telemetry and does not share data with third parties.

Removing your data

You can revoke NSync's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access or deleting the app's data from Google Drive (Drive → Settings → Manage apps) removes the synced copy.
